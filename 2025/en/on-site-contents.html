Open space

Open Space

9/27 SAT 10:20 - 16:00

Open Space is a session without a pre-determined theme. While there's a general theme and structure, the actual content is proposed by participants on the day, and groups are freely formed based on interests and needs. It's a collaborative and dynamic space for flexibly sharing knowledge and ideas. Open Space is scheduled only for Conference Day 2. Participation is simple: propose a topic on a whiteboard. Please join us in Open Space for new discoveries and inspiration! *From 12:25 to 13:20, the "PyCon JP Association Public Meeting" will be held.

Stamp Collecting

Himawari

9/26 FRI 10:30 - 9/27 SAT 16:05

To make visiting sponsor booths more enjoyable during the conference, we will be holding a stamp collecting. As with last year, participants can collect stamps by scanning the QR code located at each booth with their smartphone, accessing each company's page, and clicking "Get Stamp!" at the bottom of the page. Depending on the number of stamps collected, participants will be able to win original swags (tote bags and towels).

O'Reilly Book Sales Booth

Himawari

9/26 FRI 10:30 - 9/27 SAT 16:05

There will be a book sales booth by O'Reilly Japan, Inc. At the booth, we plan to offer: - O'Reilly publications at a 10% discount (equivalent to the consumption tax discount) - Gifts such as tote bags depending on the purchase amount

Ami Otsuka Book Sales and Autograph Session

Himawari

9/27 SAT 15:45 - 16:30

On the second day (September 27th), we will be holding a book sale and signing event for Ami Otsuka, the keynote speaker, of her book, "#100-Day Challenge: How Making 100 Apps Every Day Changed My Life." At the temporary booth on the day, Ms. Otsuka will sign the book for those who purchase it (and request it). ~Cash and PayPay payments will be accepted~
